# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AetherOS-Showcase is a technical research space. Participation should be rigorous, respectful and focused on evidence.

Expected behavior includes:

- challenging technical claims without attacking people;
- distinguishing measured facts from inference;
- acknowledging uncertainty;
- giving reproducible criticism where possible;
- respecting intellectual-property and disclosure boundaries;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats and personal abuse.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is welcome. When disputing a benchmark or architecture claim, prefer concrete counter-evidence, methodology analysis, hardware constraints or a reproducible alternative explanation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that is abusive, intentionally misleading, spammy, exposes private information, or attempts to publish sensitive security details outside the disclosure process.
